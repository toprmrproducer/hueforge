How palette generation works

HueForge converts your chosen hex color to HSL (hue, saturation, lightness), then rotates the hue around the color wheel to derive each palette. A complementarycolor sits directly opposite (180°) for maximum contrast. Ananalogous palette takes the two neighbors 30° on either side for a harmonious, low-contrast combination. A triadicpalette takes two colors 120° apart, forming three evenly spaced hues around the wheel. The image extractor samples pixels on a canvas and groups them with a small k-means pass to find the dominant colors. Every value — hex, RGB, and HSL — is computed with plain JavaScript directly in your browser tab.
