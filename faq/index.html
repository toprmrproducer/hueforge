Frequently asked questions

Is HueForge really free?+

Yes. There is no account, no paywall, and no limit on how many palettes you generate. The tool is supported by lightweight advertising.

Do my colors get uploaded anywhere?+

No. Every palette is computed locally in your browser using plain JavaScript color math. Nothing you pick ever leaves your device.

What is a complementary palette?+

A single color sitting directly opposite your chosen color on the color wheel (180 degrees away), giving maximum contrast — great for accents and call-to-action colors.

What is an analogous palette?+

The two neighboring colors 30 degrees on either side of your chosen hue. Analogous palettes are harmonious and low-contrast, good for calm, cohesive designs.

What is a triadic palette?+

Two colors 120 degrees apart from your chosen hue, forming three evenly spaced colors around the wheel — a balanced, vibrant combination.

Can I input any hex format?+

Yes. HueForge accepts 3-digit shorthand (#abc) or full 6-digit hex (#aabbcc), with or without the leading #.

Can I get a palette from an image?+

Yes. Upload any image and HueForge samples its pixels on a canvas and groups them with a small k-means pass to find the dominant colors. You can pick how many colors to extract and export the result as CSS variables, JSON, or a PNG swatch strip. The image is read locally and never uploaded.

Does HueForge work on mobile?+

Yes, HueForge runs in any modern mobile browser, including the native color picker on iOS and Android.

Every palette is generated locally in your browser. Nothing you pick is ever sent to a server.
