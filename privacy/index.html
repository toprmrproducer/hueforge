Privacy Policy

Last updated July 6, 2026

Your colors

HueForge generates palettes entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Any color you type or pick is never uploaded to any server — it is processed directly by your browser and the resulting palette is rendered on-screen. We have no access to, and keep no copy of, any color you enter.

Cookies and analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate, anonymized traffic (for example, how many people visit the site and which pages they use), and third-party advertising (such as Google AdSense) to keep the tool free. These services may use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own privacy policies.

Advertising

This site may display ads served by third-party vendors, including Google. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visitingGoogle's Ads Settings.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach out via the contact details listed on our homepage.

Every palette is generated locally in your browser. Nothing you pick is ever sent to a server.
